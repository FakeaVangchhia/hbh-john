Operator, not spectator.

John — better known online as Vøid — works the offensive side of security. The mindset is simple: to defend a system you have to understand exactly how it breaks, so he spends his time thinking like an attacker and reporting back like an ally.

Day to day that means red-team simulations, penetration testing, and living in a Kali shell with the usual suspects — recon, exploitation, and clean, actionable write-ups. When the tools don't exist yet, he builds them and ships them to GitHub.

Off the clock he's a digital creator, turning what he learns into short, sharp content that makes security feel a lot less intimidating.
